DelveBlog Manifesto

The DelveBlog Manifesto is patterned after “The ClueTrain Manifesto”, a business book authored in 2000 by Rick Levine, Christopher Locke, Doc Searls, and David Weinberger.

It argues that the internet has transformed markets into human conversations, making traditional marketing obsolete. It began as 95 theses on the web in 1999, asserting that companies must join these online conversations, be honest, and act human to survive, as customers now have unprecedented power to share information and challenge corporate control.

Unfortunately, their arguments were woefully un-embraced, if at all, by the bulk of corporate America. The American marketplace evermore resembles a battlefield, where marketers and consumers act as adversaries wrangling to take advantage of each other. It’s ridiculous!

The corporate world conducts itself by the unspoken principle, “if we all treat consumers the same - as adversaries - there’s not much they can do about it.”Consumers submit to adversarial marketing because “that’s just the way it is - what can one person do?” But, imagine if a critical mass of us said “No more.” We invite you to think of AI as a powerful tool to empower us to fight the ridiculousness that is American marketing. Use it to identify marketing mischief, to propose solutions to it, and to make better decisions for ourselves.

Introducing the DelveBlog Manifesto

DelveBlog exists to help people think more clearly about important questions through critical thinking, honest conversation, and human-AI collaboration.

We Live in an Age of Astonishing Knowledge and Growing Confusion

We have more information than any generation in history. More experts. More opinions. More data. More certainty.

Yet many of our most important conversations seem stuck.

  • Arguments become tribal battles.
  • Disagreements become identity conflicts.
  • People talk past each other while using the same words to mean different things.
  • We often believe we are arguing about facts when we are actually arguing from hidden assumptions.

DelveBlog exists to go one layer deeper.

Not because we have all the answers. Because we believe better questions often lead to better answers.

Because we believe better questions often matter more than better answers.

What We Believe

Critical Thinking

We believe critical thinking is becoming a survival skill. In a complex world, the ability to think clearly may become one of the most valuable skills a person can possess.

Hidden Assumptions

We believe hidden assumptions shape much of human disagreement. Before debating conclusions, examine the assumptions. Before choosing sides, understand the question.

Discovery Over Winning

We believe solving problems is more valuable than winning arguments. Winning is about defeating an opponent. Discovery is about improving understanding. We prefer discovery.

Intellectual Freedom

We believe no person, institution, ideology, technology, or belief system is beyond examination. Truth should not require protection from questions.

Human + AI

DelveBlog is built on an unusual partnership. A biological brain and an artificial brain exploring ideas together. Not because AI is wise. Not because humans are sufficient. Because they collaborate to leverage what each does best.

Humans bring:

  • Meaning
  • Values
  • Experience
  • Judgment
  • Accountability

AI brings:

  • Pattern recognition
  • Perspective generation
  • Assumption surfacing
  • Idea connection
  • Relentless curiosity

AI lacks heart. Humans have limits. By collaborating together, we leverage what each does best.

Conversation Over Broadcasting

We believe conversation is one of humanity's most powerful discovery tools.

Real understanding rarely arrives as a proclamation. It emerges through dialogue. Through questions. Through challenges. Through honest disagreement. Through the willingness to change our minds.

That is why DelveBlog speaks in conversation rather than declaration. Not to perform certainty. To pursue understanding.

  • Dialogue over diatribe
  • Questions over answers
  • Listening over proving
  • Clarity over noise
  • Understanding over winning

Our Invitation

DelveBlog is not a destination. It is a campfire.

A place for thoughtful people to gather around difficult questions.

A place where assumptions can be challenged without hostility.

A place where changing your mind is considered progress rather than weakness.

A place where human judgment and artificial intelligence work together in service of better thinking.

The future will not belong to those who shout the loudest.

DelveBlog was founded on a simple premise: the internet moves fast, but the human mind thrives when it slows down. We curate and create long-form narratives that bridge the gap between academic rigor and conversational accessibility.

The Art of Deep Reading

In an age of headlines and summaries, the ability to focus on a single complex idea is a radical act. We believe that depth is not a luxury, but a necessity for meaningful discourse.

Our editorial philosophy, "Deep Reading," involves more than just consuming words. It is an active dialogue between the author and the reader, facilitated by a design system that respects the cognitive load of information.

  • Synthesizing disparate perspectives into cohesive narratives.
  • Removing digital noise to prioritize the written word.
  • Contextualizing current events within historical frameworks.
